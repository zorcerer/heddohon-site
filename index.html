Heddohon

A web music player you host yourself,
for Navidrome, Subsonic and Jellyfin.

FLAC 24/192 amd64 · arm64 MIT

Install Releases Source 

browser ──► Heddohon ──► Navidrome / Jellyfin

Heddohon signs in to your music server from its own backend and proxies the audio and the artwork. The browser only talks to Heddohon, and your upstream credentials stay on the server.

01Features

  1. Original files

    Up to FLAC 24/192, with the decoded format shown in the player. Optional transcoding to MP3, Opus or AAC, switched from the quality badge. Casts to Chromecast from Chrome on Android and to AirPlay from Safari.

    24/192

  2. Coloured by the artwork

    The interface takes its accent from the cover that is playing, in two themes: Liquid (dark glass) and Paper (parchment and ink). This page does the same with the screenshots on it; the switch is in the top bar.

    2 themes

  3. Library

    Albums, artists, genres, playlists, favourites and the folders on disk, with synced lyrics and recommendations from your music server. Playlists reorder by dragging or with the arrow keys. Ratings of one to five stars on Navidrome.

    lyrics

  4. Instant mix

    Up to 100 similar tracks from a track, an album or an artist, with AudioMuse-AI's sonic similarity where it is installed.

    100 tracks

  5. Shared links and listening together

    Links to songs, albums and playlists that anyone can play without an account, for 1, 7 or 30 days. A live link plays what you play, as you play it, with reactions.

    1 · 7 · 30 d

  6. Phones and living rooms

    On a phone the navigation and the player share one panel at the foot of the screen, and the full player pulls down to close. A living-room screen shows what plays full screen with the synced lyrics.

    PWA

  7. Across devices

    The queue and settings sync between browsers. One browser can pause, skip or seek another signed in to the same account, and move the queue to it. Installs as an app on phones and desktops.

    sync

  8. Crossfade and equaliser

    Both off until switched on. Tracks that follow each other on an album get a tight handoff instead of the crossfade. The 10-band equaliser is kept per browser.

    10 bands

  9. History

    Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz through Navidrome, ReplayGain, a sleep timer. Every play is kept on your own server, summed up as top artists, albums, tracks and hours, with the albums played on this date in earlier years.

    scrobble

  10. SQLite or PostgreSQL

    SQLite by default, PostgreSQL when configured, with a one-time import from SQLite.

    db

03Install

docker compose 
git clone https://github.com/zorcerer/heddohon.git && cd heddohon
cp .env.example .env
echo "HEDDOHON_SECRET=$(openssl rand -base64 48)" >> .env
echo "HEDDOHON_SUBSONIC_URL=http://10.0.0.10:4533" >> .env
docker compose up -d
Open
http://localhost:3000, and sign in with your music server account. To expose it publicly, set ORIGIN and read SECURITY.md.
Images
ghcr.io/zorcerer/heddohon and zorcererd/heddohon
Platforms
linux/amd64 and linux/arm64: a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 on a 64-bit OS, an ARM NAS, an Ampere server. 32-bit ARM is not built.
Tags
latest is the newest release. dev is built from the dev branch once a day when it has changed and passed the test suites.
Unraid
A template is in templates/heddohon.xml.
No Docker
On Node 22 or later: npm ci && npm run build && node build/index.js
